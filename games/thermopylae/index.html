Controls: Tap Spartans to add/remove them from a group; SELECT ALL commands the whole army. Tap empty ground to move the group in formation. Move the selected group with WASD or arrows, Space to tackle, Shift to dodge incoming sling bullets; Tab selects another Spartan. On touchscreens, tap multiple Spartans then tap a destination. Press G to select everyone. Spartans may cross the wall freely; Persians slow to half speed at the wall. Click a unit or press Tab to select (including Leonidas once he arrives). Persians arrive in larger waves as the battle progresses. Slingers can be tackled and their lead bullets stun ordinary Spartans for 10 seconds. Three nearby Persians are required to defeat a Spartan. If Persians breach the wall, Leonidas enters: immune to slings, but six Persians can overwhelm him. Early Spartan casualties are replenished while the wall holds (first 90 seconds); afterward casualties are permanent. Every 30 seconds of holding the wall earns one Spartan. Pushing to the ten-yard line earns ten Spartans and Leonidas. Once the Persian army crosses the mountain pass, attackers pour in from both sides.

The hidden ten-unit advancement logic remains; there are no yard lines. Push Persia back to win, or survive as long as possible. Tactical mechanics are fictionalized; Thermopylae did not involve American football.
